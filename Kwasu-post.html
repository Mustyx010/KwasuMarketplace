Tips for a great listing
📸 Add a clear photo — listings with photos get 3× more views.
💬 Be specific in your title — include brand, model, or size.
💰 Price fairly — check what similar items sell for on campus.
⚡ Respond fast — buyers move on quickly.
Stay safe on campus
Meet buyers in public places — library, SUB, or lecture areas.
Don't share personal details beyond your WhatsApp number.
Collect payment before handing over the item.
Trust your gut — cancel if something feels off.
